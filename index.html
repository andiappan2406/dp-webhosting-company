Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about registering domains, hosting websites, and getting started with DP Host.

How fast will my domain registration and hosting be active?
Your domain registration is submitted instantly to the global registry upon checkout, with Anycast DNS propagating worldwide in under 15 minutes. All web hosting plans and VPS instances are automatically provisioned via our automated orchestration system within 60 seconds.
Is WHOIS Privacy really 100% free forever?
Yes, absolutely. We believe privacy is a fundamental digital right. Unlike legacy domain registrars who charge $10 to $20 annually, DP Host includes comprehensive WHOIS Privacy Guard at zero extra cost for all eligible top-level domains.
How does the free website migration service work?
Once you sign up for any hosting plan, simply open a migration ticket in your Client Portal and provide your current cPanel, Plesk, or FTP credentials. Our senior system engineers will migrate all your files, databases, and emails with zero downtime.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my hosting plan later?
Yes! You can scale seamlessly from Starter Cloud to Business Pro or Turbo VPS with just a single click inside your dashboard. Your billing will be prorated automatically with no downtime or service disruption.
What is your refund and money-back guarantee policy?
We offer an unconditional 30-day money-back guarantee on all cloud hosting plans. If you aren't completely delighted with the speed and reliability, you can request a full refund within 30 days—no questions asked.
Do you support WordPress, Node.js, and Python?
Yes! We provide 1-click installation for WordPress, Joomla, and Magento, as well as native runtime support for Node.js, Python, PHP 8.1 - 8.3, Ruby, and Git deployment integrations.
